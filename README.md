**[Teaching-TKA](https://rendyyxyz.github.io/Teaching-TKA/)**: four subjects, one password.

| What | Detail |
|---|---|
| Subjects | Matematika, Bahasa Indonesia, Bahasa Inggris, Fisika |
| Tryout | 30 questions, 25 of them generated so you practise the method instead of memorising the answer |
